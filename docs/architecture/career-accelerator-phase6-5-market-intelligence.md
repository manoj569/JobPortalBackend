# Career Accelerator market intelligence (Phase 6.5)

The source of demand is CareerHarbor's persisted `Job` inventory. The market worker scans jobs in bounded batches after the common ingestion stage; no ATS connector has its own extractor. It keeps a durable cursor and database lease. Reprocessing is keyed by a hash of title, description, requirements, responsibilities and structured job skills, plus the market taxonomy revision. A changed job or an approved alias can refresh its current mappings. Previous mappings remain soft-deleted historical rows.

`MarketSkill` links to the existing canonical `Skill`, and optionally to a published Accelerator competency. `MarketRole` optionally links to an Accelerator role. Public `LearningPathStatus` is `Available` only when that editorial Accelerator link exists; otherwise approved market demand is visible as `ComingSoon`. Unreviewed discovery candidates are never returned by public path/search endpoints.

The default extractor matches normalized whole phrases against canonical names and approved aliases. Acronyms shorter than three characters require a structured skill field. Unmatched structured skills, terms on explicit requirement skill lines, and distinctive technology names in skill context become collecting observations. They enter the admin review queue only after the configured distinct-job and distinct-company thresholds. A structured discovery retains its existing `SkillId` even when punctuation normalization differs, so approval does not create a duplicate canonical skill. Ambiguous role aliases remain unmapped for review. No AI provider or candidate AI quota is used.

Demand counts use the existing public-job availability rules: published, visible, non-deleted jobs at non-deleted companies, with the existing source-specific expiry behavior. Skill and role mappings are unique per active job. A previous 30-day window is used for trend only when it has at least one observed job; otherwise trend is null. Related skills mean co-occurrence in active jobs, not causation. Detail queries bound skills, companies and aliases; lists are paginated. `JobFilter` points to the existing `/api/jobs` search with an exact canonical market identifier.

Candidate recommendations use an active career goal, verified competency evidence and active market demand. Candidate evidence is read only; market calculations never change proficiency. A role gap reports a proficiency target only when the published Accelerator role taxonomy provides one. An exact candidate-matching-job count is intentionally omitted because the existing personalized matching service is not used here.

Admin review requires the existing Administrator role, an explicit reason, and audit staging. Approve, merge/map, reject and alias operations have uniqueness and revision controls. Already-approved market concepts can be merged while retaining old job mappings as historical evidence; revisioned reprocessing moves current mappings toward the surviving concept. A published Accelerator path cannot be silently merged into a different path. Backfill is bounded to 200 jobs per request. The hosted worker uses the same batch path and honors cancellation. Its logs report counts, duration and failures without descriptions or candidate content.

This pass creates no migration. The ordered Phase 3, 4, 5, 6 and 6.5 migration chain remains necessary before the worker or APIs can use these tables. PostgreSQL verification must cover SQL translation, concurrent claims, unique indexes, candidate review races and large-inventory query plans on a disposable database. The local Docker daemon was unavailable during source verification.
